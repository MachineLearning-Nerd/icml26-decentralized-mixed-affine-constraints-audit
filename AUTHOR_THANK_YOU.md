# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Demyan Yarmoshik, Nhat Trung Nguyen, Alexander Rogozin, and
Alexander Gasnikov for publishing *Complexity of Decentralized Optimization
with Mixed Affine Constraints* and for presenting a problem formulation that
connects decentralized optimization with federated learning, multi-task
learning, resource allocation, and distributed control.

The paper's separation of coupled, local, shared-variable, and consensus
constraints made it possible to build a claim-by-claim audit with explicit
operators, counters, independent KKT/LP checks, and meaningful omission
controls. The source-level discrepancy in Algorithm 2 is also documented
openly so future readers can distinguish the printed recurrence from the
source-consistent interpretation used by Appendix E.

This repository is an independent reproduction archive, not an authors'
implementation and not a substitute for the paper. We hope the preserved raw
artifacts, controls, and limitations make the work easier to inspect and
extend. Please see [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) for citation metadata.
