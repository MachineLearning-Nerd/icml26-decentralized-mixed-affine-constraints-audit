# Claim 1 method

The verifier constructs deterministic smooth strongly convex quadratics whose exact constrained optimum is fixed first, then derives the linear term and RHS so an independent KKT check is available. It executes Algorithm 1 with both graph and affine Chebyshev recurrences. Every graph-spectrum multiplication increments the communication counter.

The sweep crosses three objective condition numbers, three local-constraint condition targets, three path-graph sizes, two tolerances, and four formula-independent horizons. The main response is the observed first-hit count. Omitted-corrector and degree-one-preconditioner runs are negative controls.

Appendix G's lower bound is checked separately; no experimental trajectory is used to claim a universal lower bound.
