# Claim 4 method

Eight seeded synthetic linear-learning repetitions are generated inside the HF run. HFL and VFL equality-constrained quadratic systems are solved with a KKT system and independently compared to their centralized closed forms. MTL uses proximal gradient for the exact per-feature L2 group norm and reports an independent subgradient KKT residual. Held-out MSE establishes that each fitted model is non-vacuous; structural residuals test the application reductions directly.
