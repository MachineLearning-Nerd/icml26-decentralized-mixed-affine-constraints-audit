# Claim 3 limitations

Finite first-hit observations, including the 70-dimensional `0.001` route, do not prove the universal epsilon exponents. The objective is weighted L1 on a compact box. Paper Algorithm 2 line 12 differs from Lan's canonical outer-average recurrence, while Appendix E invokes Lan's theorem; the authoritative TeX also leaves outer `tilde u^0` undefined. The canonical route is therefore source-consistent but not text-identical. The source defect is directly certified and the natural completion is tested, but it still limits confidence to MEDIUM.
