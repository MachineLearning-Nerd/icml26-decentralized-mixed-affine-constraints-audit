# Claim 2 method

Each accepted cell contains nonzero A, C, Ctilde, and W matrices. The verifier constructs both Appendix J blocks, separately Chebyshev-preconditions them, runs Algorithm 1 on their block diagonal, and increments primitive counters on every block action. First hits are recorded at two tolerances across four independent horizons.

The independent audit recomputes both mixed condition numbers and the KKT optimum. Dropping either block is a negative control. Additive and multiplicative work models are fit only after first-hit counts are observed.
