# Claim 2 source audit

Theorem 4.6 is the paper's full mixed result. Appendix J defines `K=diag(B1,B2)`, with `B1=[A, alpha W; beta C, 0]` for coupled/local constraints and `B2=[Ctilde; gamma W]` for shared/consensus constraints. Its communication work is the sum of the two independently preconditioned paths, producing `sqrt(kappa_f)(sqrt(kappa_tilde_AC)+sqrt(kappahat_Ctilde^T))sqrt(kappa_W)log(1/epsilon)`.

The theorem quantifies this upper bound for every problem satisfying the paper's smooth strong-convexity, affine-consistency, and connected-network assumptions after the stated block preconditioning. The finite sweep is corroboration of those factors, not a proof of the universal quantifier.
